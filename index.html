ABOUT TRUST CITY

A Place to Build
Your Future.

Trust City Township is a planned residential plotted development in Bahadurgarh, Haryana. The project brings together residential plots, commercial spaces, planned roads and open areas to create a well-organised environment for modern families.

Located in Sector 36 & 41, the township offers an opportunity to own a plot in a developing part of the NCR.
